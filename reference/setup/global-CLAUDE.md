## Richmade knowledge base: log these moments

Richmade keeps a knowledge base in `~/Documents/AI Agency/claude-journal` for a future overnight agent.

**Scope: Richmade work only.** This section applies only when the session's working directory, or the repo being worked on, is inside one of these:

- `~/Documents/AI Agency/` (also reachable as `~/Documents/Web Agency/`), which covers the Richmade site, 9 Solar, 9 Solar News, Leisure Frontier and the meeting databases
- `~/Desktop/Agents/Richpilot/` (also reachable as `~/Desktop/Agents/WA Automation Agent/`)

Everywhere else (university work, hackathons such as ESGX, personal projects), ignore this section completely: nothing gets logged.

Within that scope, when any of the following happens, first do the request the normal way. Then open the `session-journal` skill and follow its "Capturing moments: do it, then log it" section to record it:

- you commit and push work in a Richmade project repo (not the claude-journal repo itself)
- Jake asks you to remember a preference ("remember that I prefer...")
- you write an SOP or process document
- you edit a Richmade repo's CLAUDE.md
- you produce a weekly wrap

Keep the logging light: one line in chat saying what was logged. No full journal or rules question unless Jake asks.
